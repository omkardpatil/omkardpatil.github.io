Underwater ROV for ship-hull inspection

A technology-transfer project between L&T and IIT Bombay, funded under the DST IMPRINT 2 programme, to design a remotely operated vehicle for inspection and surveillance at industrial level.

  • Designed a subsea camera hull rated for 50 m operation, with manufacturability in mind.
  • Ran CFD drag analysis in ANSYS Fluent on an approximate vehicle model and validated the battery hull with static structural analysis.
  • Led fabrication and mentored 5 team members selected from 150+ applicants.
  • Vehicle intended for deployment in sea water for ship-hull maintenance.

Robotics and student teams

Matsya 6, IIT Bombay AUV team
From 2021

Autonomous Underwater Vehicle: Matsya 6 and swarm

An all-student team building AUVs that localise themselves and complete realistic tasks at AUVSI RoboSub (San Diego) and NIOT-SAVe (Chennai). Guided by Prof. L. Vachhani and Prof. Hemendra Arya.

  • Designed and analysed hulls and components in SolidWorks and ANSYS Static Structural; handles achieved a safety factor above two.
  • Project Voyager: designed a 3-DOF arm with a replaceable end effector for task-specific use.
  • Project Swarm: worked on miniaturised AUVs aiming at robust underwater communication with minimal sensors, thrusters and boards.
  • Created assembly animations of Matsya 6 that won 2nd place in the Propulsion System Skills Video category at RoboSub 2021, among 50+ teams from 12 countries.
  • SolidWorks
  • ANSYS
  • Robotics
  • Underwater vehicles
