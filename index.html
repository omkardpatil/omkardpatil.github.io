From underwater robots to test rigs on the shop floor.

I’m a mechanical engineer at Bajaj Auto, where I lead durability testing in R&D. Most of my work sits where hardware meets software: instrumenting rigs, catching failures early, and removing manual effort from long-running tests.

I studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Bombay with a minor in Electrical Engineering. There I spent years on underwater vehicles, from an AUV competing at RoboSub to an ROV built with Larsen & Toubro, and that mix of mechanical design, simulation and electronics still shapes how I work.
